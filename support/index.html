Frequently asked questions

How do I book a pitch?

Open the app, find a pitch on the Discover tab (list or map), choose a date and time, and tap Book now. You can pay the full amount or split it with your teammates.

How do I join a friend’s booking?

On the Bookings tab, tap the blue “+” and enter the 6-digit code your friend shared. Add your name, pay your share, and you’ll appear on the roster.

How do payments work?

Payments are handled securely by our payment provider, Thawani. Maydan does not store your card details.

Can I cancel a booking?

You can cancel an upcoming booking from its details screen. Cancellations made more than 24 hours before the start are eligible for a refund to your original payment method; bookings starting within 24 hours are non-refundable.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Manage account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and frees your username. This action cannot be undone.

A pitch isn’t on the app — can I still book it?

Some venues aren’t on in-app booking yet. On the pitch page you can tap Call or message the venue on WhatsApp to reserve directly.

I run a venue and want to be listed

Email support@maydan.om and we’ll get you set up.
