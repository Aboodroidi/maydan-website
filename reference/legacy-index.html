About Maydan

Maydan is Oman's easiest way to find and book football pitches. Whether you're organising a five-a-side with friends or running a venue, Maydan puts the whole experience in one app — in Arabic and English.

What you can do

Everything you need to play, from finding a pitch to splitting the bill.

🗺️

Discover pitches

Browse pitches near you on a map and filter by size, surface and price.

⚡

Book in seconds

Pick a slot and confirm instantly, with secure payment in Omani Rial.

🤝

Split the cost

Pay only your share and invite teammates to pay theirs with a simple code.

📊

For pitch owners

Manage bookings, availability and earnings from one simple dashboard.
