Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Maydan (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the Maydan mobile application, which helps people discover and book football pitches in Muscat, Sultanate of Oman (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, mobile phone number (used to sign in and verify your account), username, and optional details such as your date of birth and country/dial code.
  • Location: with your permission, your device location while you use the App, to show pitches near you and centre the map. You can turn this off at any time in your device settings; the App still works without it.
  • Bookings and activity: the pitches you view and book, dates and times, party size, the names you add to a booking roster, and the 6-digit codes used to join a shared booking.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by our third-party payment provider (Thawani). We do not collect or store your full card details; we receive confirmation of whether a payment succeeded.
  • Technical and usage data: basic device information and app interactions needed to operate the service, keep it secure, and fix problems.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and verify your phone number.
  • To show nearby pitches, availability, and prices, and to process and manage your bookings.
  • To enable shared bookings (splitting cost and joining by code) and show who is on a roster.
  • To process payments through our payment provider.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • To maintain, improve, and protect the App and prevent abuse.

How we share information

  • Service providers: Google Firebase (authentication, database, hosting), Google Maps (maps and location features), and Thawani (payments). They process data on our behalf under their own terms.
  • Pitch owners / venues: when you book or join a booking, the details needed to fulfil it (such as your name and booking time) may be shared with the relevant venue.
  • Legal reasons: where required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: you can permanently delete your account and associated data from within the App: Profile → Manage account → Delete account.
  • Location: control location access in your device settings.
  • Access and corrections: contact us to access or correct your information.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. When you delete your account, we remove your account data; limited records may be retained where required for legal, security, or accounting reasons.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your data.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and revise the “Last updated” date above.

Contact us

Questions about this Policy or your data? Email us at support@maydan.om. Maydan is based in Muscat, Sultanate of Oman, and this Policy is governed by the laws of Oman.

This page is provided to meet App Store requirements and to inform our users. Please review it against applicable Omani data-protection requirements before publishing.
